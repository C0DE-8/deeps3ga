# Deep Saga 0.3 - Ant World Playtest Story Flow

Book: The Ant World: The King's Soul
Chapter: 1 - The Smallest Soul
Playtest route: observation, memory, prayer, communication, investigation, defensive instinct

## Opening

The last thing you remember is impact.

Light breaks into pieces. Breath leaves you. The human world falls away before you can understand what took your life.

Then there is pressure.

Warmth.

A wet, living dark.

Your limbs do not answer because they are not limbs in any shape your mind remembers. You try to inhale and your body only twitches, soft and segmented, surrounded by other small bodies in a chamber filled with heat, touch, and scent.

Scent rolls over you like language without words.

Hunger.
Brood.
Protect.
Move.

The meanings are not spoken. They arrive through instinct, incomplete and overwhelming.

You are alive.

You are not human.

🐜 Suggested action
Focus on the pheromone scents around you.

## Player Action 1

I focus on the pheromone scents around me.

## Game Master

You go still inside the warm dark and let the world come to you.

At first it is only smell. Then the scents begin to separate. Hunger is sharp. Brood is round and constant. Worker-attention is dry, practical, almost rhythm-like. The nursery is not a room so much as a living organ of the colony, every movement feeding into every other movement.

🧠 [DISCOVERY]
Pheromone is not speech, but it carries meaning.

A worker pauses over you. Her antennae brush your slick side.

Alive.

Strange.

Watch.

The word strange is not spoken, but it clings to you.

Far below the normal nursery scent, something bitter moves through the tunnel air and vanishes before you can decide whether it was real.

🐜 Suggested action
Observe the worker tending you.

## Player Action 2

I search my human memories for the moment I died.

## Game Master

You reach backward.

For a moment the nursery fades. You remember hard ground, broken light, the impossible heaviness of your human body, and the panic of knowing there would be no second breath.

Then the memory tears.

The ant body drags you back into warmth and scent. You have no hands to clutch your head. No voice to curse. Only a soft body that trembles among the brood while a human mind tries to fit inside it.

🌑 [MEMORY]
You remember dying, but not why you woke here.

The bitter scent returns for one heartbeat.

This time, you notice something worse: it is not random. It moves like searching.

🐜 Suggested action
Stay still and study the bitter scent.

## Player Action 3

I pray that this body grows quickly enough to survive.

## Game Master

You close whatever passes for eyes in this new body and push the wish outward.

Grow.

Survive.

Please.

Nothing dramatic happens. Your body does not split open into strength. No crown of power descends. No secret name wakes in your mind.

But the prayer does not feel empty.

For a breath, warmth gathers somewhere deeper than flesh. It is faint, distant, and gone almost as soon as you notice it, like something very far away turned its attention toward you and then looked elsewhere.

✨ [SYSTEM EVENT]
Something answered nothing you can yet understand.

The worker above you freezes. Her antennae hover over your body, uncertain.

Alive.

Strange.

Watch.

Now the scent carries a trace of fear.

🐜 Suggested action
Try to answer the worker with scent.

## Player Action 4

I try to send the same strange scent back to the worker.

## Game Master

You do not know how to speak with this body.

So you imitate.

You hold the worker's attention in your mind and push toward the feeling she gave you: alive, strange, watch.

The result is clumsy. Too much hunger. Too much panic. Too much human fear folded into an insect signal.

The worker recoils half a body-length.

Not pain.

Not rejection.

Recognition.

🐜 [COLONY RESPONSE]
The worker knows you tried to answer.

She touches you again, slower this time, and the nursery around you tightens with alertness. Somewhere beyond the brood chamber, legs scrape against packed earth.

The bitter scent moves again.

Closer.

⚠️ Suggested action
Focus on where the bitter scent is coming from.

## Player Action 5

I stay still and study the bitter scent below the nursery smell.

## Game Master

Stillness becomes your first weapon.

You let the colony's normal scents pass over you: food, brood, worker, warmth, damp soil.

Underneath them is the wrongness.

It is thin and bitter, like a needle hidden in cloth. It does not spread like panic. It does not pulse like hunger. It threads through the air in careful lines, pausing, retreating, returning.

Searching.

Not for food.

Not for larvae.

For something specific.

🌑 [MYSTERY]
Something has entered the nursery, and it is looking for something.

The worker tending you turns toward the tunnel.

For the first time since waking, you understand one clean thing:

you are helpless, but you are not useless.

🐜 Suggested action
Warn the worker as best you can.

## Player Action 6

If something touches me, I bite with everything this body has.

## Game Master

The instinct arrives before courage does.

Your human mind imagines teeth, muscles, a body lunging forward.

Your larval body offers less.

A twitch.

A curl.

A tiny mouthparts' threat against a world far larger than you.

But the intent is real.

If the thing searching this nursery reaches you, you will not simply wait to be taken.

⚔️ [INSTINCT]
Fear becomes the beginning of resistance.

The worker lowers her head over you, not quite shielding you, not quite studying you. Her scent changes.

Protect.

Strange.

Important.

Somewhere in the tunnel, the bitter thread stops moving.

For one breath, the nursery is silent.

Then something scratches softly from the dark.

🐜 Suggested action
Listen before you move.
