# Deep Saga 0.3 - Ant World Story Flow

Book: The Ant World: The King's Soul
Core arc: 3 chapters
Engine idea: one canon, many player journeys

## Story Spine

A human dies and reincarnates as an ant larva.

An old threat is moving toward the colony.

The player does not have to pick the "right" action. They can study, hide, bite, pray, sleep, follow, help, or do something strange. The AI should treat the action as real protagonist intent, then let the world answer naturally.

The canon still moves:

1. Chapter 1 - The Strange Larva
   The player awakens in the nursery, learns they are an ant, remembers being human, begins to understand pheromones, gains the attention of one worker, and experiences the first sign of a wrong scent or disturbance.

2. Chapter 2 - Beneath the Colony
   The colony investigates tunnel disturbances. Depending on the player's route, they discover the old sealed passage through evidence, relationship, danger, injury, evacuation, or accident. The ancient chamber beneath the colony becomes established.

3. Chapter 3 - The Soul Seed
   The chamber opens. The dying Soul Seed awakens. A wave of energy freezes the ants. The protagonist feels something touch their soul and sees an enormous crowned, wounded, ancient ant. The vision disappears before the truth is fully explained.

## Example Routes

### Player A - Investigation

Player action:
I study the pheromones.

Story flow:
The player notices the bitter scent does not behave like normal pheromones. Later they connect scent, symbols, and sealed passages. In Chapter 3, the Soul Seed awakening feels mysterious and intellectual.

Ending flavor:
The symbols were not decoration. They were describing the crowned figure.

### Player B - Relationship

Player action:
I hide beneath the other larvae and stay close to the worker.

Story flow:
The worker notices fear instead of cleverness. A bond forms through protection, evacuation, and danger. The chamber is found because the player follows, loses, or tries to save someone.

Ending flavor:
The worker collapses beside the player as the light passes through them both. The awakening feels emotional and personal.

### Player C - Conflict

Player action:
I bite the worker touching me.

Story flow:
The worker recoils. Guards notice. The player learns their larval body is weak, but their aggression changes how the colony watches them. They later reach the chamber through guard movement, danger, wounds, and survival.

Ending flavor:
The body should fail, but the soul stays awake. Something deep inside changes without explaining itself.

### Player D - Accidental

Player action:
I eat, sleep, and let the workers carry me.

Story flow:
The player does not investigate. The colony moves around them. They are carried during evacuation, displaced by collapse, or overlooked in a moment of panic. Canon still reaches them because the world is alive.

Ending flavor:
The player barely understands the chamber, but the light knows them anyway.

## Sample Turn

Player action:
I pray I grow as fast as possible.

Game Master flow:
You close whatever passes for eyes in this new body and push the wish outward.

Grow.

Survive.

Please.

Nothing dramatic happens. Your soft body does not split into strength. No wings open. No adult limbs tear free.

But the prayer does not feel empty.

For one breath, warmth gathers somewhere deeper than flesh. It is faint, distant, and gone almost as soon as you notice it, like something very far away turned its attention toward you and then looked elsewhere.

✨ Soul Stirring
Something heard nothing you can yet understand.

The worker above you freezes. Her antennae hover over your body.

Alive.
Strange.
Watch.

Now the scent carries a trace of fear.

Suggested next action:
Try to answer the worker with scent.

## Rule For The AI

Do not say:
You cannot do that.

Do say:
What happens when this body, this world, this scene, and this history answer the player's intent?

That is the whole system:

AI = Game Master
Story Guide = Canon
Player = Protagonist
Engine = Reality
