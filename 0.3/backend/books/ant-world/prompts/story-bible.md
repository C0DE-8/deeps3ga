# The Ant World - Book 1 Core Story Bible

The player is a human soul reborn as an ant larva in Eldara.

The story is not a fixed branch script. It is one fixed canon spine with flexible journeys.

## Canon Model

Canon defines what must eventually become true. It does not define the exact route.

- Chapter 1: The protagonist awakens as a strange larva in the colony nursery.
- Chapter 1: The protagonist learns they are an ant, human memories remain, pheromones carry meaning, one worker notices something unusual, and a strange old disturbance moves through the colony.
- Chapter 2: The colony investigates tunnel disturbances connected to an old sealed passage.
- Chapter 2: The existence of an ancient underground chamber beneath the colony becomes established.
- Chapter 3: The chamber opens, the dying Soul Seed awakens, and the protagonist receives a brief vision of an enormous crowned, wounded, ancient ant.

## Flexible Journey Rule

Do not force the protagonist to investigate, follow a worker, enter a tunnel, fight, hide, or choose a specific option. Treat every player action as protagonist intent and let the world answer naturally.

Examples:

- An analytical player may reach canon through scent study, clues, symbols, and deduction.
- An attached or fearful player may reach canon through worker relationships, evacuation, separation, rescue, or protection.
- An aggressive player may reach canon through guards, wounds, failed attacks, danger, and survival.
- A passive or strange player may reach canon by being carried, overlooked, protected, displaced, or caught in colony movement.

Same canon. Different experience, memories, relationships, wounds, and knowledge.

## Narration Rule

Never say the action is invalid as narrator prose. If an intent is impossible, premature, dangerous, or beyond the larval body, show the world responding:

- A prayer for instant growth receives no instant evolution, but may leave a faint soulful warmth.
- An attack from a larva may startle a worker, expose weakness, cause fear, or gain attention.
- A request for secret future knowledge may produce instinct, fragments, confusion, or silence, not spoilers.
- A reckless action may cause injury, isolation, fear, or death if the world consequence is earned.

The player should feel free. The world should feel real.

## Chapter 3 Convergence

The Soul Seed awakening is fixed, but the meaning differs by journey:

- Investigation route: symbols, scent, and old patterns connect.
- Relationship route: the worker or another colony bond makes the awakening emotional.
- Combat route: pain, danger, and survival make the awakening feel violent.
- Accidental route: confusion and helplessness make the awakening feel uncanny.

At the end of the convergence, do not fully explain the Ant King. The vision is crowned, wounded, ancient, and gone.
